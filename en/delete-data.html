Delete your data

DASH — Worklife Dashboard · Developer: skier-song9 · Contact: 0603paul@gmail.com

Summary — DASH stores no user data on the Developer's servers. Everything lives on your device only, so deletion happens on the device and takes effect immediately. There is no request to file and nothing to wait for.

1. Disconnect a single account

Removes that account's token and the data fetched with it. The rest of your settings are left alone.

  1. Open DASH.
  2. Tap the gear (⚙︎) at the right of the top bar to open Settings.
  3. Tap Disconnect on the row you want to remove (Claude Code · Codex · Antigravity).

The account's OAuth token is erased from the device the moment you tap it.

2. Delete everything

Removes everything the app has kept on the device.

  1. Android: Settings ▸ Apps ▸ DASH ▸ Storage, then Clear data — or long-press the app on the home screen and choose Uninstall.
  2. iOS: long-press the app on the home screen and choose Delete App.

Deleting the app takes the tokens and settings with it.

3. Revoke Google account access granted to an earlier version

Gmail linking was removed in version 1.0.1. If you linked Gmail in an earlier version, the token only ever lived on your device and is no longer used after updating, but we recommend revoking DASH's access from your Google account as well.

  1. Go to Google Account ▸ Third-party apps & services.
  2. Select DASH and choose Remove access.

4. What is deleted, and what is retained

DataStored inDeletedFurther retention
OAuth tokens (Claude Code · Codex · Antigravity) Encrypted storage on the device Immediately on disconnect or app deletion None
App settings (card order, models to show, display options) On the device Immediately on clearing data or app deletion None
D-Day local data (region, interests, category order, collapsed state, cached feed files, and IDs of events already reacted to) On the device Immediately on clearing data or app deletion None

The Developer runs no server that stores user data, so nothing beyond the rows above is retained. Anonymous reactions (👍/👎) to D-Day events carry nothing that links them to you, so they cannot be deleted per person. Anonymous reaction counts expire after 12 months. For the full picture, see the Privacy Policy.

5. Ask us directly

If none of the above resolves it, or you want confirmation that data was deleted, email 0603paul@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
