Feature 02

Unread sidebar

A vertical panel attached to the right of the usage dashboard. For every Gmail account you've linked it shows the unread count, and below it keeps a few lines — only the mail worth looking at now. When you don't need it, fold it away and the whole screen goes back to the dashboard.

Fold and unfold // a sidebar that doesn't steal the screen

Press the › next to the title and the sidebar folds away, leaving a thin rail at the edge. Press the ‹ on that rail to bring it back. The dashboard lays itself out again to fit the width that's left.

Account settings

Personal paul@example.com Edit ›
Work work@example.com Edit ›

The sidebar above really folds and unfolds. Press the gear (⚙) and it switches to account settings. Both buttons are reachable with Tab and work with Enter/Space.

  • Account line — colour dot, label, email address, unread count. Anything above zero is shown in red.
  • Mail line — sender, one line of subject, when it arrived. Press the check button on the right and it's marked read in Gmail right away.
  • Folded list — three lines per account by default; the rest open up with "Show N more".

New mail announces itself // without you looking at the screen

DASH stands on the desk beside you. A count going from 3 to 4 is only ever noticed by looking straight at it, so when mail arrives the screen edges flash three times in that account's colour. The colour alone, caught out of the corner of your eye, tells you which account it landed in.

With several accounts they take turns. Mail in Personal (green) and Work (blue) means green three times, then blue three times. Blending the two into one flash would leave a colour that belongs to neither.

Waiting to announce: nothing

Press a button and the edges flash three times in that account's colour, and its count goes up by one.

  • Three flashes — shorter than the usage warning's five. Mail arriving is news worth knowing about, not a limit about to land that you have to act on now.
  • The account's own colour — the same one it wears in the sidebar. Change the colour and the flash changes with it.
  • Mail that was already there does not flash — what was sitting unread when you opened the app, a mail you marked read here that Gmail keeps returning for a poll or two, and a mail whose verdict landed late are all not arrivals.
  • It tints inward from the edges, so it never paints over the gauges or the list, and after three flashes it is gone completely.

Gear → account settings // the same spot, a different screen

Press the gear next to the sidebar title and the same panel turns into the account list. No new window opens, and the screen doesn't take you somewhere else. The gear becomes a back arrow (‹) so you can return to the mail list.

Each account line has Edit › on the right. That takes you into the settings for that one account — label, colour, and its important-mail criteria.

Labels and colours // telling accounts apart at a glance

Email addresses are long and look alike. So each account gets a short label and a colour dot. Pick a label from Personal · Work · School or type your own, and pick one of eight colours. It shows up in the sidebar the moment you choose.
