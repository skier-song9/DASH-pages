Privacy Policy

Effective: August 6, 2026 · Revised: September 6, 2026 · Contact: 0603paul@gmail.com

DASH (the "App") is an iOS and Android application provided by an individual developer (GitHub: skier-song9, the "Developer"). This policy explains what information the App processes, for what purpose — and what it does not process. Both platforms offer the same features and process the same information for the same purposes; where they differ (the name of the encrypted store, for one) both are named below. This is a translation provided for convenience; if it conflicts with the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

Summary — the App stores no user data on the Developer's servers. Usage data is processed and kept on your device only, and the App talks to nothing but each AI service's own usage API.
Revised September 6, 2026 (version 1.0.1) — Gmail linking and the Unread sidebar have been removed from the App. From this version on, the App processes no Google account information or Gmail data, sends nothing anywhere for mail triage, and sends no error reports to the Developer. If you linked a Google account in an earlier version, the token only ever lived on your device and is no longer used after updating; you can revoke DASH's access yourself in your Google account security settings.

2. Purposes of use

  • Reading each service's usage API to display AI coding tool limits.
  • Forecasting when quota runs out from how usage changes. This is computed on the device only.

3. Storage and security

  • All data is stored on your device. The Developer operates no server that stores user data.
  • OAuth tokens are held in the encrypted store the operating system provides — the Keychain on iOS, and EncryptedSharedPreferences, keyed by hardware-backed keys, on Android.
  • All network traffic is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS).
  • The App contains no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

4. Third parties and processors

The Developer does not provide or sell user data to third parties, and delegates processing to no one. The only parties the App communicates with are the usage APIs of the AI services you linked yourself (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google), and each request carries nothing beyond the token that service issued.

5. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data is deleted when you delete the App.
  • Disconnecting an account in the App's settings immediately deletes that account's token.

Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your data page.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Changes to this policy

Changes will be posted on this page; material changes will be announced in the App.
